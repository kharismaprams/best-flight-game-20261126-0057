    DAWN RUN BRIEFING

    The mist is lifting over the Lantern Archipelago. Six keepers are waiting on their lights, and the little post plane is fueled and ready.

    ⭕
    Thread all 30 rings — follow the glowing course from island to island. Every 6th ring earns a bonus lantern.
    🏮
    Deliver to all 6 keepers — drop a lantern (Space) near each keeper's beacon to light their island.
    🛬
    Land home at Home Strip — mind your fuel and the crosswind. Leftover fuel becomes bonus points.

    HOW TO PLAY

    You fly the dawn mail route in chase view. The brass arrow at the top of the screen always points to your next ring.

    Steer the plane↑↓ pitch · ←→ bank · AD rudder. The mouse steers too.
    Boost & dropShift boost (burns fuel faster) · Space drop a lantern over a keeper's island.
    Charts & pauseM route planner · Esc pause menu · R restart the run.
    ScoringRings +80 · deliveries +220 · shore lanterns +15 · leftover fuel ×4. Win runs to unlock hangar upgrades.

    HANGAR & UPGRADES

    Postmaster Rue fits one new part after every completed route. Fitted parts stay on the plane for good.
